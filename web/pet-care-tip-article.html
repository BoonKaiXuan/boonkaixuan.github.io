How to ‘loose leash walk’ your dog

Getting started with loose leash walking

Loose leash walking means your dog walks calmly beside you without pulling.It helps make your walks more enjoyable and less stressful for both you and your dog. Start in a quiet place where your dog feels comfortable. Hold the leash loosely and let your dog stay close to your side. Use treats or praise when your dog walks nicely without pulling. This helps your dog learn that staying near you is a good behavior.Be patient and consistent. With time, your dog will learn to walk calmly with you.

Build a better bond with your dog

Walking together is not just exercise, it is also a way to build trust. When your dog feels safe and guided, they will listen to you more. Avoid pulling the leash or getting angry. Instead, stop walking when your dog pulls and continue when they relax. Practice every day, even for a short time. Small progress will lead to better behavior over time. A calm walk creates a happy dog and a stronger connection between you and your pet.
