FAQ

Is the chewable weight toy safe for dogs?
Yes, this chewable weight toy is made from safe and non-toxic materials.It is designed for everyday chewing and play. However, we recommend supervising your dog during use, especially if your pet is a strong chewer, to ensure safety at all times.
What toys are best for cats?
Cats usually prefer lightweight and interactive toys such as balls, feathers, or string toys. Colorful ball set help improve their hunting instincts and keep them active. It is important to choose toys that are safe and the right size for your cat.
Can this toy help with my dog’s teeth?
Yes, chewing on this toy can help improve your dog’s dental health.It helps reduce plaque buildup and keeps teeth cleaner. At the same time, it also provides mental stimulation and helps reduce boredom and stress.
